# Poster Block — image recipe

Fill the `[SUBJECT]` slot and leave everything below it alone. The constraints
are what make three unrelated subjects come back looking like one family.

```text
[SUBJECT: describe the subject in one clause — e.g. "a stack of shipping containers", "a single ceramic cup", "an abstract network of nodes"]

Rendered as flat vector illustration or silkscreen-style poster art.

STYLE — apply all of the following, they are not optional:
- flat spot colours with hard edges and no gradients
- limited palette — cream #FBFBEF, mustard #F5CE3E, forest #0F6B4C, coral #D96A5E
- cut-out photographic elements at mismatched scales, hard-masked
- subtle paper grain overlay
- bold overlapping geometric shapes

NEGATIVE SPACE: One flat band of solid colour must stay empty for the headline.

DO NOT INCLUDE: soft shadows, gradients, 3D rendering, photorealism, glass or blur, muted or corporate palettes.
```

**Target —** provider, model and resolution are all configurable; set them to
whatever image stack you are using.

- provider: `configurable — set to your image provider`
- model: `configurable — set to your image model`
- resolution: `1536x1024 (3:2 landscape); use 1024x1536 for portrait, 1024x1024 for square`

## Hard constraints

- flat spot colours with hard edges and no gradients
- limited palette — cream #FBFBEF, mustard #F5CE3E, forest #0F6B4C, coral #D96A5E
- cut-out photographic elements at mismatched scales, hard-masked
- subtle paper grain overlay
- bold overlapping geometric shapes
- negative space — One flat band of solid colour must stay empty for the headline.
- never include legible text, logos or UI chrome in the generated image

## Negative prompt

```text
soft shadows, gradients, 3D rendering, photorealism, glass or blur, muted or corporate palettes
```

## Worked examples

Three unrelated subjects, same style:

**1 — Festival**

> a crowd silhouette cut out against flat colour

**2 — Coffee**

> a hard-masked cut-out of a filter cone

**3 — Cycling**

> a cut-out cyclist mid-turn at mismatched scale
