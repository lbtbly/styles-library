What early access includes

Three things, finished. Nothing half-built.

We would rather ship a small tool that works on your real calendar than a large one that works in a demo.

Protected blocks

Two to four hours a day held before anyone else can reach them, rescheduled automatically when your week moves.

The polite refusal

A decline that offers two real alternatives, written in your voice, so declining stops costing you a paragraph each time.

An honest weekly

Friday afternoon: how many hours you actually got, what took them, and which recurring meeting is quietly eating your month.

How do you decide which hours to protect?

From your last eight weeks: when you accept meetings, when you decline them, when your commits and documents are actually written. The first suggestion arrives within an hour of connecting, and you can move it.

Which calendars work?

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 at launch. CalDAV is in the second cohort. We do not support standalone Apple Calendar yet and will not pretend otherwise.

What do you do with my calendar data?

We read event times, attendee counts and acceptance state. We do not read event bodies, attachments or documents, and nothing is used to train anything. Disconnect and the copy is deleted within 24 hours.

What will it cost?

Nine dollars a month per person after the trial. Everyone in the first three cohorts keeps that price permanently, including if their team grows.
