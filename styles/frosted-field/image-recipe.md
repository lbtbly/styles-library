# Frosted Field — image recipe

Fill the `[SUBJECT]` slot and leave everything below it alone. The constraints
are what make three unrelated subjects come back looking like one family.

```text
[SUBJECT: describe the subject in one clause — e.g. "a stack of shipping containers", "a single ceramic cup", "an abstract network of nodes"]

Rendered as soft 3D render or gradient abstraction with frosted glass elements.

STYLE — apply all of the following, they are not optional:
- pale periwinkle-to-ice gradient ground
- translucent frosted panels with visible blur of what sits behind them
- one muted violet #5D51C8 and one seafoam #69C6AE as the only hues
- soft omnidirectional lighting, no hard shadows
- rounded organic forms, generous corner radii

NEGATIVE SPACE: The upper half stays a clean uninterrupted gradient.

DO NOT INCLUDE: hard edges, high contrast, dark backgrounds, neon, sharp specular highlights, dense detail.
```

**Target —** provider, model and resolution are all configurable; set them to
whatever image stack you are using.

- provider: `configurable — set to your image provider`
- model: `configurable — set to your image model`
- resolution: `1536x1024 (3:2 landscape); use 1024x1536 for portrait, 1024x1024 for square`

## Hard constraints

- pale periwinkle-to-ice gradient ground
- translucent frosted panels with visible blur of what sits behind them
- one muted violet #5D51C8 and one seafoam #69C6AE as the only hues
- soft omnidirectional lighting, no hard shadows
- rounded organic forms, generous corner radii
- negative space — The upper half stays a clean uninterrupted gradient.
- never include legible text, logos or UI chrome in the generated image

## Negative prompt

```text
hard edges, high contrast, dark backgrounds, neon, sharp specular highlights, dense detail
```

## Worked examples

Three unrelated subjects, same style:

**1 — Finance**

> a soft translucent stack of rounded cards

**2 — Weather**

> a frosted glass sphere holding pale mist

**3 — Wellness**

> smooth overlapping pebbles in pale water
